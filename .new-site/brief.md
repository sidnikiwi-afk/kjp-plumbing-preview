# Brand Brief

## Positioning
- **Business:** KJP Plumbing & Heating — Powerflush Specialists in Southampton & surrounding areas
- **Ideal Customer:** Homeowners in Southampton SO postcodes needing emergency plumbing, powerflushing, bathroom installs, or heating repairs
- **Primary CTA:** Request a Quote (secondary: Call Now — 02381 11 2994)
- **Differentiator:** 25+ years experience, powerflush specialists, 24/7 emergency response, UK Enterprise Awards winner 5 years running
- **Positioning Statement:** KJP is Southampton's go-to local plumber — emergency-ready, powerflush specialists with 25+ years of experience and a wall of 5-star Google reviews. Not a franchise, not a call center — Kyle and his team, on your doorstep.

## Brand Voice & Tone
- **Personality:** Direct, reliable, friendly
- **Tone:** Straight-talking tradesman, no jargon, no overselling
- **We are:** Local, experienced, fast, affordable, specialist
- **We are NOT:** Corporate, salesy, flashy, generic

## Visual Identity

### Color Constraints
- **Anchor colors:** None strict — Kyle wants a new design. Current site uses dark navy + red.
- **Avoid:** Nothing specified

### Font Constraints
- None — designer's choice per concept.

> Final palette and fonts are selected per concept direction during brainstorm.

### Visual Style
- **Keywords:** Trustworthy, bold, local, professional, emergency-ready
- **Inspiration patterns:** Current Squarespace site is dark navy with red accents, service icon cards, Google reviews, FAQ accordion, contact form, massive SEO location footer
- **Do:** Keep it feeling like a real tradesman's site — confident, not corporate. Highlight the reviews and credentials.
- **Don't:** Generic template look, stock photo overload, overly corporate blue
